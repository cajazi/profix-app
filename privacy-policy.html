ProFix Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 02, 2026

This Privacy Policy for ProFix Technologies (doing business as ProFix) describes how we collect, use, and share your personal information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect: names, phone numbers, email addresses, usernames, government ID documents, selfie/profile photo, bank account details, billing addresses, and device information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: create and manage your account, process payments via Paystack, verify your identity (KYC), send notifications, enable user-to-user communications, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Information We Share

We share your data with Paystack for payment processing only. We do not sell your data. We may share with Nigerian regulatory authorities if required by law.

4. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as you have an account with us. Upon account deletion, we remove your data except where legally required to retain it.

5. Security

We use encryption, bcrypt password hashing, and secure cloud storage to protect your data. All data is encrypted in transit using TLS.

6. Children

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18 years of age.

7. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at support@cosmas.dev

8. Push Notifications

We may send push notifications about your account. You can turn these off in your device settings.
